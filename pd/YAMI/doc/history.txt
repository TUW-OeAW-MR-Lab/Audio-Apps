*** DEVELOPMENT STATUS ***

*** v1.4 (02.07.2024) by Michael Mihocic
- YAMI16adc has been discarded, and replaced by YAMI24adc24
- summary of current YAMI patches:
	> YAMI		  ( 24 DAC channels,  4 ADC channels)
	> YAMI2		  (  2 DAC channels,  2 ADC channels)
	> YAMI100	  (100 DAC channels,  2 ADC channels)
	> YAMI24adc24 ( 24 DAC channels, 24 ADC channels)

*** v1.3 (01.09.2020) by Michael Mihocic
- Play.pd: Bug fixed when cancelling synchronous stimulation and 
	stimulating again immediately
- bug fixed in YAMI100: Play/Stop was not connected

*** v1.2 (24.04.2020) by Michael Mihocic
- bug fixed in YAMI100: pla88 fixed to play88 on 3 positions
	caused problems when stimulating more than 88 channels

*** v1.1 (17.03.2020) by Michael Mihocic
- bug fixed in YAMI100: play41 and play42 were defined twice
- documentation updated (YAMI100)

*** v1.0 (09.2019) by Michael Mihocic
- pd upgraded to v0.49
- ExpSuite FW upgraded to v1.0
- YAMI index increased to v1.0
- flext added (to sources only), and compiled, required to compile libraries
- libraries updated and compiled: 
	> vasp -> loadbang warnings removed
	> dyn ->  loadbang warnings removed; 
		bug fix: multiple, dynamic loading of tracker.pd prevented by 
		using a spigot object -> check if tracker has already been loaded,
		multiple instances of comport object (same port) cause errors
	> comport -> index starts with 1 instead of zero ->
		YAMI patches updated to stay backwards compatible:
			+ Tracker load: default COM-port: 1 instead of 0
			+ Tracker init: [input value + 1] instead of [input]
- GEM & ViWo removed
- testYAMI folder cleaned up
- testtone.pd adapted to 96 channels
- available YAMI patches:
	> YAMI		( 24 DAC channels,  4 ADC channels)
	> YAMI2		(  2 DAC channels,  2 ADC channels)
	> YAMI100	(100 DAC channels,  2 ADC channels)
	> YAMI16adc	( 24 DAC channels, 16 ADC channels)
